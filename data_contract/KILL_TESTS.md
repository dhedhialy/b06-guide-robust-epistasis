# KILL TESTS

Stop / narrow / reframe on any of these (Sec 28):

1. Realistic efficacy bounds make nearly every interaction UNRESOLVED.
2. No public dataset has enough independent guide multiplicity.
3. Target-expression proxies are causally contaminated, no independent calibration exists.
4. Robust signs do not beat conventional signs in guide-held-out prediction.
5. Off-target sensitivity eliminates nearly all robust calls.
6. The exact sharp-bound result is independently published before submission.
7. The method reduces to a known estimator with different notation.

A negative result is still publishable as a design theorem: minimum efficacy
calibration precision required for sign identification.
